charts/wire-ingress: The Envoy proxy fleet created by this chart now defaults to 3 replicas with advisory node/zone spread (`gateway.envoyProxy.replicas`, `.topologySpreadKeys`). A PodDisruptionBudget with `minAvailable: 1` is created automatically unless the final replica count is 1. New opt-in `gateway.zoneAwareRouting.enabled` makes Envoy prefer backend pods in its own availability zone, cutting inter-AZ traffic cost on multi-zone clusters; its default `minEndpointsThreshold: 3` enables the preference for three-endpoint backends. New `gateway.annotations` and `gateway.infrastructure.labels`. See the wire-ingress README.
