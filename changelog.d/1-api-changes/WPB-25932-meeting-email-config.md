Meeting invitation/update/cancel email rendering and transport moved from galley to brig: configure brig's `emailSMS` instead of the legacy `galley.config.settings.meetings.email` block. For backward compatibility the legacy galley block is still honored: its presence enables meeting emails and its `replyTo` feeds brig's `emailSMS.general.meeting.replyTo`; transport/sender/branding keys under it (`from`, `useSES`, `aws.*`, `smtp.*`, `templateDir`, `branding`) are ignored. The new keys are `galley.config.settings.meetings.emailsEnabled` and `brig.config.emailSMS.general.meeting.replyTo`.
